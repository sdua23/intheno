How this works

Players. The page includes all players who played in the last full season. A player with 500 or more minutes gets a grade. Players with less than 500 minutes are in the section Not graded. The incoming draft class is in that section too. Age is the age on July 1 of the offseason. Guards are point guards and shooting guards. Wings are small forwards. Bigs are power forwards and centers. Each player has the position from that season. Years of service is the number of NBA seasons in which the player played, including the last season. A season with no games, for example because of injury, does not count. This is different from the service count in the collective bargaining agreement.

Tiers. Each player is ranked against all players in the league, not only against his position. Thus, a position with few good players shows a low count. Each tier is a fixed share of the league. For example, Starter / key reserve and better is always about 180 players. Thus, the charts show how a fixed number of good players is divided by position and age. The charts do not show if the league has more or less talent than before. Total WAR gives a second measure that is not a count. Wins above replacement uses LEBRON with a replacement level of −1.40. Raw rate uses the rating only. With raw rate, a player who missed games does not lose his grade.

History. Each bar is compared only with the same bar in earlier offseasons. Later offseasons are not used. A bar gets a color only when there are four or more earlier offseasons. The incoming class contains all picks from the draft that summer. It also contains players who did not play in the NBA. These players have no position. The data does not include the 2026 draft yet. For 2026, the list shows players on 2026-27 rosters who have no NBA data.

Limits. The charts show the talent that is available now. It is not a forecast. Tests of forecasts are a different part of this project.

Data: LEBRON from BBall Index. Minutes and positions from the player-season file of Gabe. Birth dates, draft history and 2026-27 rosters (Oct. 8, 2026) from Basketball-Reference. SkillCurve, In The N.O.
