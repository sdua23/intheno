Choose one or more defenders and see what their teammates need to provide to reach your defensive target. Compare that requirement with real NBA supporting casts, then see how teams with similar defenders performed.

Ratings are measured in points per 100 possessions. Positive is better than league average; negative is worse.

How it works

Each player’s defensive rating averages five season-centered metrics: defensive LEBRON, DPM, predictive EPM, xRAPM and RAPM. Percentiles are calculated within each season among players with at least 500 minutes, covering 4,678 player-seasons.

The calculator adds player ratings together. Minute-weighted totals explain 91% of the variation in team defensive rating within the same season. That fit is partly built in because the plus-minus metrics use the same games. Using previous-season ratings, the figure is 34%, with a slope of 1.17.

Supporting casts

The comparison uses the five players credited with the most possessions on each team’s end-of-season roster across 390 team-seasons. For the open slots in your lineup, it checks every possible group of the required size from those five players. A group meets the target when its combined rating covers the remaining requirement.

Possession totals come from repaired stint data and include fractional possessions. Garbage time was removed upstream.

Historical comparisons

The calculator looks for teams whose 1,500-minute rotation included defenders rated as poorly as each weak defender you selected and as highly as each strong defender you selected. Ratings of zero or higher count as strong for this comparison.

These results describe what happened. They do not isolate what those defenders caused. Poor defenders often play on rebuilding teams, which can affect the comparison.

Defensive targets

Top-10 and top-5 targets use the average defensive-rating gap from league average at those ranks, converted to the player-rating scale using the 1.17 slope.

The calculator does not estimate wins or dollar values because the defensive component of replacement level has not been settled.
