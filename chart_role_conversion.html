Cheap Flier Conversion by Role
filled = redeemed · outline = cheap fliers signed (bar length = group size)
Denominator: first-stint underperformers who became true cheap fliers (year-5 salary under 8% of cap) and received a real second chance (an 800+ minute season in year 5 or later), grouped by year-4 listings. Redemption = a starter-level season (xRAPM ≥ +1) in year 5 or later. Hover any bar for the names. Cells under five players are marked small-sample in the hover — including Creators (0-for-2), who convert well overall but are almost never available at flier prices, so their zero here is scarcity, not failure. Defensive roles are year-4 listings: note the hits who became Wing Stoppers (Goodwin, Moody, Okogie) started elsewhere — defensive promotion was the mechanism, not the starting point.
